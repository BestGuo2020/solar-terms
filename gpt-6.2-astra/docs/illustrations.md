# 节气插画来源与绘制提示

本项目使用内置 ImageGen 绘制节气插画，作为物候和农事的地域性示例。当前节气卡与详解使用同一幅作品，构图与纹理随项目提供。

沿用的四幅画分别是立春的梅花溪谷（`public/art/spring.webp`）、小暑的夏日荷塘（`public/art/summer.webp`）、秋分的秋色山谷（`public/art/landscape.webp`）与小雪的薄雪松林（`public/art/winter.webp`）。

其余 20 幅逐幅独立生成，保存在 `public/art/terms/`。每幅的完整提示由下面的通用前缀、表中对应场景与末尾约束依次组成。

**通用前缀**

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a dedicated panoramic header illustration in a Chinese solar-term educational atlas. Compose a single wide 3:1 landscape painting, edge to edge. Refined Chinese gongbi botanical detail with blue-green shanshui landscape, fine ink outlines and woodblock hatching, subtle warm silk paper grain, restrained mineral pigments and natural seasonal light. Distinct focal motif centered in the middle horizontal band, clearly identifiable even when cropped to a narrow web card. Paint this particular seasonal vignette:
